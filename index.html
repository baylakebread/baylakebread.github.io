Why it tastes different

Three ingredients and a very long wait.

Every loaf starts with a live sourdough starter we feed by hand, then ferments slowly so the flavour has time to build and the bread is easier on your gut.

01

A living starter

A live starter does the lifting instead of packet yeast. That is what gives sourdough its tang and its keeping quality.

02

Slow fermentation

Long, cool rises instead of shortcuts. That is where the open crumb, the blistered crust and the flavour come from.

03

Simple ingredients

Flour, water, salt, and real butter, fruit or cheese when a recipe calls for it. Clean, wholesome, nothing hidden.

04

Small batches

We bake what we can do well, by hand, in our own kitchen. If it sells out, it sells out, and the next drop comes soon.

How to order

We bake in drops. Here is how it works.

Instead of keeping a shop open every day, we open a batch for orders, bake it fresh, and you collect it from the Bread Shed.

  1. Watch for the drop

    We announce each menu on Instagram and by email. Subscribe on our order page so you do not miss one.

  2. Place your order

    Pick your loaves and treats online, choose a pickup slot, and pay in one go. Popular items sell out fast.

  3. Collect it fresh

    Come by the Bread Shed on pickup day. Everything is bagged, labelled and still smelling like the oven.

Our story

It started as a weekend project with my daughter.

What began as a fun hobby with Hailey turned into a small-batch bakery full of heart, flavour and good-for-your-gut goodness.

We bake out of our own kitchen in St. Cloud and serve neighbours here and at the Harmony farmers market. Everything is mixed, shaped and scored by hand, which is why no two loaves look exactly the same, and why we would not have it any other way.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

How do I keep my bread fresh?

Keep it at room temperature in a paper or bread bag and cut from the middle out, storing it cut side down. Do not refrigerate it. To keep a loaf longer, slice it first and freeze it, then toast straight from frozen.

How do I store and reheat my focaccia?

Store at room temperature in an airtight container for up to 2 to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 5 days. For longer storage, freeze it tightly wrapped for up to a month. To reheat, warm it in a 300°F oven for 5 to 10 minutes, or microwave for 10 to 15 seconds until soft and warm.

Do you make anything that is not sourdough?

No, because we love the health benefits and the taste of sourdough. It is the heart of everything we bake.

How far ahead do I need to order?

Order as soon as a drop opens, because the favourites go quickly. Focaccia needs about 48 hours’ notice, so message us ahead for that one.

Which areas do you serve?

St. Cloud and the greater Orlando area. Pickup is at the Bread Shed on the day announced with each drop, and you will also find us at the Harmony farmers market.
